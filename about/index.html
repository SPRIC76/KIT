About

Keep it Ticking

Hassle-free awake modes that keep your PC, Apps & AI ticking.

KiT is a quiet Windows companion that keeps your session awake (Light) or darkens the desktop without sleeping the PC (Night), with wakeable screensaver faces; Off returns ordinary Windows.

Single instance, MIT. No telemetry, no account, no cloud, no keystroke recording, no clipboard access, no fake input. SHA-256-verified releases on GitHub. Two launches: installer (current-user scope) and portable (no admin needed). Winget path is in motion.

One program. Entirely local. No accounts. No telemetry. No theater (except the screensavers).

Why it exists

Most “stay awake” tools do one trick: wiggle the mouse, or hold a key down, and hope nothing notices. KiT is built as an instrument instead. It asks Windows for what it needs the proper way, shows you exactly one thing (which mode you are in), and gets out of the way. Night is the part nobody else bothered with: a dark, wakeable desk that keeps the machine working while the room goes quiet.

It is simplistic on purpose and robust underneath: reliable, consistent, efficient, extremely lightweight, and effective. Those are the standards every release is held to.

Three modes

Light — The PC and display stay awake. Quiet and ready.

Night — Wakeable dark — Screen darkens, the machine stays working. A touch of activity brings you back.

Off — Regular old sleepy Windows again.

One gesture each. No schedules. No idle. No hassle.

The Instrument

A low-profile bar sits just above the taskbar — there when you want it, gone when you don't. Double-click the tray icon to show or hide it. Right-click the tray for settings, Ghost, Hold Night, Auto after idle, and the Night faces.

Ctrl + Shift + D Light · Default mode
Ctrl + Shift + K Night · Auto-wakes on mouse/keyboard activity
hold either for Off · triple-tap to flip to the opposite mode (Light → Dark, Dark → Light)

Optional Ghost — transparent, click-through Control Bar/UI Element. OptionalHold Night — stay dark until you say otherwise. Esc or Space leaves Night Hold. Optional Auto after idle — from Off or Light, enter Light or Night after 1–30 quiet minutes. Optional run at sign-in. Remembers how you left it.

Night screensavers/faces: Globules/Kit Commander, DVD, Snake, Orbit — or quiet Off. Burn-safe motion. Built to feel alive, not busy.
