Keep it Ticking

Hassle-free awake modes that keep your PC, Apps & AI ticking.

KiT is a quiet Windows companion that keeps your session awake (Light) or darkens the desktop without sleeping the PC (Night), with wakeable screensaver faces; Off returns ordinary Windows.

One program. Entirely local. No accounts. No telemetry. No theater (except the screensavers).

Three modes

One gesture each. No schedules. No idle. No hassle.

The bar shows exactly one thing: which mode you are in. Click a segment, press a chord, or leave it to the tray.

Light

The PC and display stay awake. Quiet and ready.

Ctrl + Shift + D · Default mode

Night

Wakeable dark — Screen darkens, the machine stays working. A touch of activity brings you back.

Ctrl + Shift + K · Auto-wakes on mouse or keyboard activity

Off

Regular old sleepy Windows again.

Hold either chord for about two seconds

The Instrument

There when you want it, gone when you don't.

A low-profile bar sits just above the taskbar — there when you want it, gone when you don't. Double-click the tray icon to show or hide it. Right-click the tray for settings, Ghost, Hold Night, Auto after idle, and the Night faces.

Toggles & keybinds

  • Ctrl + Shift + DLight · Default mode
  • Ctrl + Shift + KNight · Auto-wakes on mouse/keyboard activity
  • Hold eitherOff · keep the chord down for about two seconds
  • Triple-tap eitherFlip to the opposite mode (Light → Night, Night → Light)
  • Esc or SpaceLeaves Night Hold

Chords only. KiT registers the shortcuts with Windows and never reads what you type.

Optional, all off by default

  • Ghost

    Transparent, click-through Control Bar. The instrument stays visible without ever being in the way.

  • Hold Night

    Stay dark until you say otherwise. Esc or Space leaves Night Hold.

  • Auto after idle

    From Off or Light, enter Light or Night after 1–30 quiet minutes.

  • Night brightness

    Choose how deep Night goes, from a soft dim to fully dark, for Windows and, where the monitor allows it, the panel itself.

  • Run at sign-in

    Start with Windows, per user, no service and no scheduled task. Remembers how you left it.

  • Updates only when you ask

    Tray → Check for updates… refreshes the copy you are already running, straight from GitHub Releases. Nothing else touches the network.

Night faces

Built to feel alive, not busy.

Night screensavers/faces: Globules/Kit Commander, DVD, Snake, Orbit — or quiet Off. Burn-safe motion. Pick one from the tray, or let Random choose. A touch of activity ends the show unless you asked it to hold.

Trust

Quiet. Efficient. Under your control.

No account. No cloud dashboard. No “engagement.” Trust is earned by staying small and saying no.

What it does

  • Holds the session awake in Light and Night
  • In Night, clears the desk and darkens the display; a touch of activity — or Escape — can return you, unless you choose Hold Night
  • Offers global hotkeys for Light, Night, Off, and flip — chords only, never a record of what you type
  • Draws its Night faces and control bar locally
  • Checks for updates only when you choose Check for updates…
  • Runs as a single instance

What it never does

  • Record keystrokes or clipboard
  • Fake mouse or keyboard input
  • Store, send, or type credentials
  • Call home, collect telemetry, or open the network except that opt-in update path
  • Demand administrator rights for ordinary use

FAQ

Short answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. KiT is free and MIT-licensed. There is no account, no tier, and nothing to unlock.

Does KiT move my mouse or press keys to stay awake?

No. It asks Windows to keep the session and display awake through the ordinary power request, and it never fakes mouse or keyboard input. Nothing you type is read.

What is the difference between Night and turning the monitor off?

Night keeps the machine working while the desk goes dark, so downloads, renders, and long-running jobs finish. A touch of activity brings you straight back; Hold Night keeps it dark until you say otherwise.

Does it need administrator rights?

No. The installer is current-user scope and the portable runs from any folder. KiT never demands administrator rights for ordinary use.

Windows SmartScreen showed a warning. Is the file bad?

Windows may look once at a new publisher. That is ordinary, not a verdict. Check the SHA-256 digest beside the download if you want certainty. If a locked-down PC refuses to run it, that is publisher policy, not a corrupt file.

Where do settings live, and how do I remove everything?

Preferences sit in %LOCALAPPDATA%\KiT. Uninstall from Settings → Apps, or delete the portable file. Delete that folder if you want a truly clean slate.

Does it work with more than one monitor?

Yes. Night covers the whole desk, every attached display, and the faces size themselves to the monitor they are shown on.

How do updates work?

Only when you ask. Tray → Check for updates… looks at GitHub Releases, and if there is a newer build it replaces the copy you are running. There is no background checker.

Which platforms?

Windows 10 and 11 today. Other platforms are being explored; the aim is the same quiet instrument everywhere.
