How it works

Three modes. One gesture each.

A slim bar above the taskbar shows the one thing that matters: which mode you're in.

Light

PC and screen stay awake. Quiet and ready.

Ctrl+Shift+D

Night

Screen goes dark, the machine keeps working. A touch brings you back.

Ctrl+Shift+K

Off

Ordinary Windows, sleep and all.

FAQ

Short answers.

Is it really free?

Yes. No account, no tier, nothing to unlock. It's closed-source freeware; the license says exactly what that means.

How is Night different from turning the monitor off?

Night keeps the machine working while every display goes dark, with no buttons to find. A touch of the mouse or keyboard brings you straight back, or Hold Night keeps it dark until you press Esc or Space. To save power, pick Monitor off in the tray's Screensaver (Night) menu: Night then turns the displays off, and a touch still brings them back.

Will my PC still lock or sleep when I want it to?

Yes. Switch to Off, or hold either chord, and Windows goes back to its own power settings.

Windows SmartScreen warned me. Is the file bad?

Windows looks twice at new publishers; that's ordinary, not a verdict. Compare the SHA-256 digest with the one on the release page if you want certainty.

How do I remove it completely?

Uninstall from Settings → Apps, or turn off Run at sign-in and delete the portable file. Settings live in %LOCALAPPDATA%\KiT (%LOCALAPPDATA%\KiD if you ran it under its earlier name); delete that folder for a clean slate.
