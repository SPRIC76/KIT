## Three modes. One gesture each.

| Mode | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Light** | The PC and display stay awake. Quiet and ready. |
| **Night** | Wakeable dark — Screen darkens, the machine stays working. A touch of activity brings you back. |
| **Off** | Regular old sleepy Windows again. |

One gesture each. No schedules. No idle.  No hassle.

### The Instrument

A low-profile bar sits just above the taskbar — there when you want it, gone when you don’t. Double-click the tray icon to show or hide it. Right-click the tray for settings, Ghost, Hold Night, Auto after idle, and the Night faces.

<img src="https://spric76.github.io/KIT/img/tray-menu.png" width="320" alt="KiT's real right-click tray menu: Light, Night, Off, Hide control bar, Ghost bar, Hold Night, Run at Windows sign-in, Auto after idle, Night brightness, Monitor brightness, Screensaver (Night), Check for updates and Quit">

### Toggles/Keybinds

| Keys | Mode |
|---|---|
| **Ctrl+Shift + D** | Light · Default mode |
| **Ctrl+Shift + K** | Night · Auto-wakes on mouse/keyboard activity |

Hold either 2 seconds for Off · double-tap to flip to the opposite mode (Light → Night, Night → Light). Prefer other keys? Pick your own Light and Night keys from the tray's Keys item.

### Set it once, then forget it

- Optional **Ghost** — transparent, click-through Control Bar/UI Element.
- Optional **Hold Night** — stay dark until you say otherwise. **Esc** or **Space** leaves Hold Night.
- Optional **Auto after idle** — from Off or Light, enter Light or Night after 1, 5, 10, 15 or 30 quiet minutes.
- Optional **Night brightness** — how deep Night goes, from a soft dim to fully dark.
- Optional **Monitor brightness** — a convenience for displays with DDC/analog brightness controls; sets the panel's own brightness from the tray instead of reaching for its buttons, and does nothing on displays without DDC (most laptop panels).
- Optional run at sign-in.

Remembers how you left it.

## Short answers.

<details>
<summary><strong>Is it really free?</strong></summary>

Yes. No account, no tier, nothing to unlock. It's closed-source freeware; the [license](LICENSE) says exactly what that means.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How is Night different from turning the monitor off?</strong></summary>

Night keeps the machine working while every display goes dark, with no buttons to find. A touch of the mouse or keyboard brings you straight back, or Hold Night keeps it dark until you press Esc or Space. To save power, pick Monitor off in the tray's Screensaver (Night) menu: Night then turns the displays off, and a touch still brings them back.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Will my PC still lock or sleep when I want it to?</strong></summary>

Yes. Switch to Off, or hold either chord, and Windows goes back to its own power settings.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>Windows SmartScreen warned me. Is the file bad?</strong></summary>

Windows looks twice at new publishers; that's ordinary, not a verdict. Compare the SHA-256 digest with the one on the release page if you want certainty.
</details>

<details>
<summary><strong>How do I remove it completely?</strong></summary>

Uninstall from Settings → Apps, or turn off Run at sign-in and delete the portable file. Settings live in `%LOCALAPPDATA%\KiT` (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\KiD` if you ran it under its earlier name); delete that folder for a clean slate.
</details>
